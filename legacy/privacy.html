Privacy Policy

Night Files by VanCasTech · Last updated October 7, 2026

Night Files is a personal content tool that creates original short horror-story videos and uploads them to the operator's own TikTok account. This policy explains what information the app handles when you connect a TikTok account.

Information we collect

When you log in with TikTok and approve access, TikTok gives the app:

  • Your basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, and avatar), under the user.info.basic permission.
  • Information about the public videos on your TikTok account (such as video IDs, titles or captions, cover images, share links, and posting times), under the video.list permission. This is listing information only; the app does not download your original video files through this permission.
    • \n
  • An access token and refresh token that let the app read your public video list and upload videos to your account, under the video.upload and, if granted, video.publish permissions.

The app does not collect your password, contacts, messages, location, viewing history, or any other personal data.

How we use it

The information is used only to upload videos generated by the app to the connected TikTok account, either as drafts or as posts, to check whether an upload succeeded, and to identify and keep track of the public videos that already exist on the connected account. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private automation environment that only the operator can access. Generated videos and captions are kept for a short time (up to 7 days) for troubleshooting, then deleted automatically.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. Data is sent only to TikTok, as needed to read your public video list and upload your videos.

Your choices

You can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. After that, the app can no longer access your account. You can also email us to ask for any stored data to be deleted.

Children

The app is not directed at anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
