Privacy Policy

Kindled Iron · Last updated October 11, 2026

Kindled Iron is a personal publishing tool that uploads original short videos to the operator's own channel accounts. This policy explains what information the tool handles when the operator connects a Google (YouTube) account.

Information we access

When the operator signs in with Google and approves access, Google gives the tool an access token and refresh token under the youtube.upload permission. This lets the tool upload videos to the connected YouTube channel and set their title, description and settings.

The tool does not read your email, contacts, files, watch history, subscriptions, comments, or any other Google data, and does not collect passwords or location.

How we use it

The tokens are used only to upload videos made for the Kindled Iron channel to that channel, and to confirm that an upload succeeded. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private automation environment that only the operator can access. They are never published or shared.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information. Data is sent only to Google, as needed to upload videos.

Google API Services

Kindled Iron's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the tool can no longer upload. You can also email us to ask for any stored data to be deleted.

Children

The tool is not directed at anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
