Short videos that answer one Bible question at a time

Kindled Iron makes short vertical videos that answer common questions about God and the Bible, with every quoted verse shown on screen from the World English Bible (public domain).

This site describes the small publishing tool the channel's owner uses to upload Kindled Iron videos to the channel's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads and Facebook accounts. The tool is used only by the channel's owner; it has no public sign-up.
