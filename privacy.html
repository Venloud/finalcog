Privacy

Final Cog by VanCasTech · Updated November 20, 2026

The current app runs on your own computer. It stores videos and scheduling data locally and sends a video to TikTok only when you authorize an inbox-draft upload.

Information stored on your computer

The local app stores uploaded video files, filenames, captions, hashtags, optional caption variants, scheduled times, settings, and upload status in the local data folder. It also stores your TikTok account identifier and basic profile information needed to show the connected account.

TikTok access and refresh tokens are encrypted in the local SQLite database using the secret in your `.env` file. Keep that secret private and stable. Other local app data is not encrypted by this app. Anyone with access to your computer or data folder may be able to read it.

Information sent to TikTok

When you connect TikTok, the app uses TikTok Login Kit to request `user.info.basic` and `video.upload`. The server exchanges authorization codes and refreshes tokens. When a scheduled item is due, the app uploads the selected video using TikTok's inbox-draft flow and checks its processing status.

The app does not publish videos publicly. TikTok's inbox upload endpoint does not receive the caption or hashtags from Final Cog; those stay in your local library for you to copy into TikTok while reviewing the draft. TikTok handles uploaded videos and account information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Local operation and retention

Final Cog's first version uses a local Node.js server, SQLite, and files in `data/`. The scheduled-upload worker runs only while that server process is running. This build does not use Replit Auth, Replit Database, or Replit-hosted storage for the app's local data.

Videos and queue history stay on your computer until you remove them or delete the app's data folder. Disconnecting TikTok removes saved authorization tokens and sessions but leaves your videos and queue data in place. Back up the database and uploads folder together if you need a backup; protect the backup like the original files.

Website services

The static marketing page loads type from Google Fonts and photographs directly from Pexels. Those providers may receive ordinary browser connection information when the page loads. The app does not include analytics or advertising tracking.

Security and your choices

Use the app only on a computer and account you control. Keep `.env`, the data folder, and backups private. You can disconnect TikTok in the app or revoke Final Cog's access from your TikTok connected-app settings. Revoking access does not remove videos already sent to TikTok or posts published there.

To remove all locally stored app data, stop the server and remove the `data/` folder. To remove an individual video, first remove or finish its active queue item, then use the library's remove action; this also removes that video's queue history.

Other platforms and children

Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts are not connected in this version; their optional caption variants remain local. Twitch is not connected. Final Cog is not directed to children under 18.

Changes and contact

This policy will change if data handling changes. Questions about this policy can be sent to van02.vlcb@gmail.com.
