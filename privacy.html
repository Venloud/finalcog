Privacy

Final Cog by VanCasTech · Updated November 20, 2026

The app runs on your computer. Optional AI caption drafts send selected video frames to Anthropic only when you request them.

Information stored on your computer

The local app stores uploaded video files, captions, caption variants, schedules, settings, and status in its data folder. TikTok account details and session data are also stored locally.

TikTok tokens and saved app credentials are encrypted at rest. A generated key is kept in a user-only local secrets file. Video files and ordinary library data are not encrypted; protect your computer, data folder, and backups.

Information sent to TikTok

When you connect TikTok, the app uses TikTok Login Kit to request `user.info.basic` and `video.upload`. The server exchanges authorization codes and refreshes tokens. When a scheduled item is due, the app uploads the selected video using TikTok's inbox-draft flow and checks its processing status.

The app does not publish videos publicly. TikTok's inbox upload endpoint does not receive the caption or hashtags from Final Cog; those stay in your local library for you to copy into TikTok while reviewing the draft. TikTok handles uploaded videos and account information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Optional AI captions

AI caption drafting is off by default. If you add your own Anthropic API key and request a draft, Final Cog sends up to three frames from the selected video and any transcript you enter to Anthropic. The app does not automatically transcribe audio. Anthropic processes those requests under its own terms and privacy policy; review the generated text before using it.

GitHub imports

When you search or import a GitHub release or Actions artifact, Final Cog sends the repository, release, or run request to GitHub. Imported files are copied into your local library. An optional GitHub token is kept in your local environment configuration.

Local operation and retention

Final Cog uses a local Node.js server, SQLite, and files in `data/`. The scheduler runs only while that server process is running. The app binds to the local computer by default.

Files and queue history stay on your computer until you remove them or delete the app's data folder. Disconnecting TikTok removes its saved authorization tokens and session but leaves videos and queue data in place. Back up the database, local secrets file, and uploads folder together.

Website services

The marketing page serves its fonts locally and uses no externally sourced photographs. The app does not include analytics or advertising tracking.

Security and your choices

Use the app only on a computer and account you control. Keep the local secrets file, optional `.env`, data folder, and backups private. You can disconnect TikTok in the app or revoke access in TikTok settings. Revoking access does not remove videos already sent to TikTok.

To remove all locally stored app data, stop the server and remove the `data/` folder. To remove an individual video, first remove or finish its active queue item, then use the library's remove action; this also removes that video's queue history.

Other platforms and children

Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts are not connected yet; their saved caption variants remain local. Twitch is not connected. Final Cog is not directed to children under 18.

Changes and contact
