Privacy Policy

Final Cog by VanCasTech. Updated October 27, 2026.

Final Cog is a developing video publishing and scheduling product. This policy describes the current prelaunch website and how account connections would be handled if and when publishing integrations become available. The earlier Night Files policy has been preserved separately.

Information we handle now

The present demo allows you to select local video files, preview them, draft captions, and plan sample publishing times. Demo settings and draft metadata may be held in your browser's local storage; selected video files are previewed in your current browser session. The demo does not currently upload those videos to Final Cog servers, create accounts, authenticate social media accounts, or publish content. The early access contact action opens your email application; it is not a hosted signup form.

Social account integrations

Planned integrations may let you voluntarily connect TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and other supported services. If activated, and only after you grant permission, the integration may process the connected account's platform identifier, basic profile information, video identifiers, captions, media metadata, publishing status, and OAuth access or refresh tokens to carry out features you authorize. Permissions and available features will depend on the platform and its approval process. We do not ask for your social media passwords.

For TikTok specifically, previously implemented Night Files integrations requested basic profile information, public video listing metadata, and, where authorized, upload or publishing permissions. Any future Final Cog integration must be configured and documented against the actual scopes approved before activation.

How we use information

We use the information necessary to preview drafts, prepare user-directed posts, check delivery results, avoid accidental duplicate posts, troubleshoot failures, and support connection or deletion requests. We do not sell personal information or use your connected account data for advertising profiling.

Storage and protection

At present, demo drafts are browser-local. Production integrations are not offered through this demo. If enabled later, authentication tokens should be held securely with access controls, not exposed in public pages or repositories; posting state and necessary operational logs may be retained to prevent duplicate uploads and diagnose errors. Specific production retention periods will be published before those services launch. Do not rely on the former Night Files seven-day media retention statement as a guarantee for Final Cog.

Sharing and service providers

When publishing integrations become operational, data may be transmitted to the social platforms you choose as necessary for your authorized actions, and to infrastructure providers required to operate the service. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can clear browser-local demo data using your browser's site-data controls. For connected platform accounts when integrations become available, you can revoke access in the platform's security or connected-app settings. To ask about access or deletion of information under our control, contact us at the address below. Revocation does not erase posts already published to a third-party platform; manage those posts directly on the platform.

Children

Final Cog is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly seek their personal information.

Changes

As the service develops, this policy will be updated to describe actual features and handling practices. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
