FAQ

Good to know.

Does Final Cog publish videos publicly?

No. The first TikTok integration sends videos to your inbox drafts. You review the draft and complete publishing inside TikTok.

Which channels can I connect today?

TikTok is the first integration. Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts are coming soon; Twitch is planned for later.

Can I change a scheduled time?

Yes. Move a queued video from the queue or calendar. Final Cog also keeps a minimum gap between TikTok inbox uploads.

What happens when TikTok rejects an upload?

The item is marked failed or needs review with the reason when available. Final Cog does not automatically resend an upload TikTok processed or flagged.

Where are my videos stored?

The first version runs on your own computer. Video files, captions, queue details, and encrypted TikTok tokens stay in the local data folder until you remove them.
