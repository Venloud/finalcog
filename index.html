05 / FAQ

Good to know.

Does Final Cog publish videos publicly?

No. TikTok uploads go to inbox drafts for your review.

Which channels work today?

TikTok works now. Instagram and YouTube are coming soon; Twitch later.

Can I change a scheduled time?

Yes. Reschedule any queued video; minimum spacing still applies.

What if TikTok flags an upload?

It needs review and will not be resent automatically.

Where are my videos stored?

Video files and queue data stay in your local data folder.
