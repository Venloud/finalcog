03 / ANSWERSFAQ

The details.

Is Final Cog already publishing to TikTok? +

Not yet. You're viewing the product's landing page while we build and validate the publishing service. Public automatic posting will depend on TikTok approval and account authorization.

How will automatic scheduling work? +

You'll choose your timezone and weekly posting slots. Videos will automatically fill the next available slots. You can still reorder, edit, pause, or schedule an individual post manually.

Is there a free plan? +

Free signup is part of our product plan, but account creation and pricing are not live yet. You can contact us to ask about early access.
