Night Files is a social publishing app that connects to an authorized TikTok account. It can access basic TikTok profile information to identify the authorized account, read the account's public video list so existing posts can be identified and tracked, upload videos to TikTok drafts, and publish videos directly to the authorized account.

  1. Connect your TikTok account. Night Files sees your basic profile information (name and avatar) to identify the authorized account.
