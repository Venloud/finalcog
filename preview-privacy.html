Website Privacy

Final Cog landing page, October 23, 2026

What the preview handles

The landing page displays public information and interactive design elements. Switching between the TikTok, YouTube and Instagram preview options does not connect any account or send anything to those platforms.

Email

The early access form uses a mailto link that opens your email application with a draft addressed to the operator. The form does not submit or store your email address on Final Cog servers. If you send the message, your email provider and the recipient process it in the usual way.

Hosting

GitHub Pages, if enabled for this repository, may receive technical data like your IP address, browser details and requests to serve the website. See GitHub's privacy notice for details. The external Google Fonts service may receive requests to deliver the typography used on this page.

Accounts and uploads

Creator accounts, media uploads, TikTok OAuth, and production publishing are not enabled on this landing page. Before these features launch, this notice must be updated to accurately describe authentication, video storage, account connections and deletion practices.
