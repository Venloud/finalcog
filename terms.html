Final Cog by VanCasTech · Updated November 20, 2026

Final Cog is a local-first tool. You run the server, manage the local files, and decide when to finish publishing a TikTok draft.

What Final Cog does

The current app organizes video files, captions, hashtags, schedules, and upload status on your computer. When authorized, it sends videos to TikTok inbox drafts. It does not auto-post videos publicly. You must review the draft and complete publishing inside TikTok.

The local scheduler works only while the Node.js server is running and the computer has network access. A TikTok upload may fail, need review, or be delayed by TikTok. Final Cog does not automatically resend an upload TikTok has processed or flagged.

Your content and accounts

You are responsible for the videos, captions, hashtags, and account you use. You must have the rights and permissions needed to upload your content and comply with TikTok's terms, community rules, and applicable laws.

Connecting TikTok requires your authorization. Keep your local `.env` and data folder private. You are responsible for protecting your computer, backups, and any person who can access the local app. You can disconnect TikTok from the Accounts screen or revoke access in TikTok settings.
